Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

This policy covers the private apps and tools that Jack Venberg builds and runs for himself, his household, and groups he helps manage, such as a homeowners' association (together, "the apps"). The apps are not offered to the public. Some sign in with a Google or Microsoft account to work with that account's data, with your permission.

What the apps access

  • Only the data an app needs to do its job, using the permissions you approve when you sign in (for example, read-only access to email, calendars or files).
  • Your account's name and email address, to confirm which account is signed in.

How data is used

  • Only to provide the app's features to you, such as searching, reading, organizing or saving your own data.
  • Never sold, used for advertising, or used to build profiles.
  • Never used to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine learning models.
  • Some apps work with an AI assistant you choose to use. When you ask the assistant to act on your data through an app, the data needed for that request is sent to the assistant's provider, only to complete the request and under that provider's terms.

Where data is stored

  • The apps run on your own device or in cloud accounts Jack controls. Data is kept only as long as an app needs it for its purpose.
  • Sign-in tokens are kept in secure storage, such as the macOS Keychain, and are never shared.

Sharing

Data is not shared with anyone else, except service providers needed to run an app (such as hosting), when you direct it, or when required by law.

Google user data

The apps' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • Revoke an app's access at any time in your Google account or Microsoft account.
  • Email the address below to ask what an app holds about you, or to have it deleted.

Changes

Updates are posted on this page with a new effective date.
